# Facebook Reels upload sheet (Meta Business Suite / Facebook Creator Studio)

Same files as YouTube: the vertical `.mp4` (1080×1920, under 60 s) is posted as a **Reel**.

**Where:** business.facebook.com → **Create reel** (or Content → Create → Reel) → upload the `.mp4`.

**Every Reel:**
- **Captions:** Edit → **Captions** → upload the `.srt` (English). If the `.srt` won't load, turn on auto-generated captions and check them.
- **Cover:** pick a frame where the year and the country name both show (around 6–8 s in).
- **Audio:** keep the original audio. Don't add music from Facebook's library: it would play over the narration.
- **AI label ("AI info"):** the narration voice is synthetic (Kokoro TTS) and the pictures are drawn in code. Meta asks for a label on realistic AI-made video or audio. These are stylised, but the voice sounds real, so decide for yourself (switching it on is the safe choice).
- **Schedule:** Share options → **Schedule** → the date below, **5:00 PM**.
- **After it goes live:** post the first comment below and pin it.

Facebook has no title or tag fields for Reels. The caption does that job, so keep its first line as the hook, with 3–5 hashtags at the end.
