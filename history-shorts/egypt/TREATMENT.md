# Egypt in 60 Seconds — Treatment

Series: History in 60 Seconds (@PrintedHistory) · Silkscreen Travel Poster · 1080×1920, 24 fps, 59.7 s.

| | Japan | UAE | Egypt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | contrast phrase | surprising fact | a list of three wonders paid off by one river ("Pyramids. Cleopatra. The Suez Canal. All thanks to one river: the Nile.") |
| Cold open | train slides across | fall down the tower | sideways glide past the pyramids, easing out to the full print |
| Base | volcano, hills | dunes, rocky ridge | stepped desert plateaus, green banks, the Nile with drifting glints |
| Signature | registration snap | seven-ink fountain | **the route stamped segment by segment** along the Suez Canal, one rising harp note per stamp |

| Snap | 1964 | 1971 union | the Aswan High Dam |
| Instruments | koto, shamisen, taiko | oud, darbuka, ney | harp, sistrum, frame drum, ney (Kurd → Nahawand) |

Eras: 3100 BC two lands unite (pharaoh in the double crown) · 2560 BC the Great Pyramid (and a camel caravan) · 1279 BC Ramesses II (seated colossi, a sun shaft crossing the facade) · 332 BC Alexander to Cleopatra (the Pharos lighthouse) · 641 Islam and Cairo (domes and minarets) · 1869 the Suez Canal (route stamp, a steamer) · 1952 revolution and the High Dam (pylons, snap) · today (Cairo at night, the tower, feluccas, pyramids across the river) · the wall of prints swaying on the line.

Voice notes: "Ramesses" is spoken as "Ramses" (Kokoro stressed the long form oddly); "carves" became "cuts" (sounds like "calves" in a British accent).
