# Greece in 60 Seconds — Treatment

Series: History in 60 Seconds (@PrintedHistory) · Silkscreen Travel Poster · 1080×1920, 24 fps, 54.6 s.

| | India | USA | Greece |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | talks to the viewer | a quiz question | **a myth first** ("Legend says two gods competed for one city. Athena won it, with an olive tree."), paid off when the Parthenon is dedicated to Athena |
| Cold open | descend in an arc | punch-in | **a spiral out**: tight and turned 14° on the temple, unwinding level as it pulls back to the whole print |
| Base | monsoon sky, terraces, banyan | prairie, mountains, windmill | **the Aegean**: wind-combed cloud streaks, a big low sun in the middle with its road on the water, a deep sparkling sea, rocky islands, a rocky shore with silver olive trees, a sailing caique, gulls |
| Signature | brush-painted zero | rocket-plume wipe | **the boustrophedon wipe**: in 508 BC the new print is written in row by row, left to right then right to left (as the ox ploughs, like the earliest Greek writing), a Greek-key ribbon trailing a stylus; it plays in its own pause |
| Silence | 1858 | 1861 | 1460: the dance stops on "Ottoman"; a lone lyra phrase falls over the slack harbour |
| Comeback | 1930 | 1903 | 1830: the key turns major and a bouzouki chord lands as the tall ships sail in |
| Instruments | sitar, tabla, tanpura | banjo, fiddle, bass, washboard | **bouzouki (tremolo), Cretan lyra (bowed), daouli and toubeleki** in a **7/8 kalamatianos** (3+2+2); D minor with a raised 4th → D major from 1830 |
| Ending | "What surprised you most?" | "Did you get the quiz right?" | **"Would you have voted in ancient Athens? Tell us below."** — card "YOUR / VOTE?" |

Eras: 1900 BC Knossos (a terraced palace, red columns tapering downward, horns on the roofline, a grand stair, dolphins leaping) · 508 BC democracy (the assembly hill, a speaker on the stone platform, citizens raising hands) · 438 BC the Parthenon (the temple on its rock, light moving across the columns, an olive tree, an owl) · 334 BC Alexander (a phalanx with long pikes, a horseman, galleys rowing, dust) · 1460 (dusk, a fortress on a rock, one boat rocking at anchor) · 1830 (tall ships under full sail, a white chapel with a swinging bell) · 1896 (the marble stadium packed with people, runners, a torch) · today (a white island village, blue domes, windmills turning, a ferry) · the wall of prints.
