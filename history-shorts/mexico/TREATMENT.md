# Mexico in 60 Seconds — Treatment

Series: History in 60 Seconds (@PrintedHistory) · Silkscreen Travel Poster · 1080×1920, 24 fps, 52.3 s.

| | USA | Greece | Mexico |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | a quiz question | a myth first | **a hidden-city mystery** ("Under Mexico City lies another city, built on an island in a lake."), answered in 1325 |
| Cold open | punch-in | spiral out | **a spring pull-back**: from close on the island temple, it springs back past the full view, settles, and pushes on steadily into the first print |
| Base | prairie, windmill | the Aegean | **blue agave fields** in rows, nopal cacti swaying, a two-layer sierra, a row of fat cumulus, a ringed sun high on the left, vultures circling, a hummingbird darting |
| Signature | rocket-plume wipe | boustrophedon wipe | **papel picado**: in 1810, five strings of cut-paper banners drop down the screen in turn and the new print shows through them as they grow; it plays in its own pause |
| Silence | 1861 | 1460 | 1521: the music stops on "falls"; one low marimba roll fades under smoke and falling ash |
| Comeback | 1903 | 1830 | 1810: a rising marimba roll while the banners drop, a full guitar strum as they land; the key turns major |
| Instruments | banjo, fiddle | bouzouki, lyra, 7/8 | **marimba (rolls), nylon guitar, guitarrón, maracas, a box drum** in **sesquiáltera** (6/8 and 3/4 alternating); A minor → A major from 1810 |
| Ending | "Did you get the quiz right?" | "Would you have voted?" | **"Have you been to Mexico? Tell us below."** — card "BEEN / THERE?" |

Eras: 1200 BC the Olmecs (a colossal helmeted head, palms, workers with ropes, a raft) · AD 200 Teotihuacan (the stepped Pyramid of the Sun, its stair, pilgrims climbing, smoke from the top) · 1325 Tenochtitlan (a twin-shrined temple on an island, floating gardens, canoes) · 1521 (a broken temple, smoke, falling ash) · 1810 (a village church, bells swinging, banners, a crowd with lanterns) · 1910 (riders in wide hats galloping, dust, cacti) · 1920s (a mural appearing panel by panel, the painter's scaffold moving along) · today (a city with a tall tower, a column with a golden winged figure, jacarandas shedding blossom, traffic) · the wall of prints.

Engine changes: `papel: true` (film.js `papelWipe`, 1.2 s sweep); `cold.bounce` [x, y, zoom]; a silence era's longer sweep now starts earlier so it still ends as the print lands (its label stamps stay out of the sweep); AD 200 uses `at: null` + `year_at`, so the print lands before "Around the year" and the year stamps on "200".

Voice notes: "Teotihuacan" is spelled "Tayo-teewa-kahn" in the voice text (/teɪəʊtiːwəkɑːn/; whisper writes it back as "Teotihuacan"); "Tenochtitlan" came back as "to notched it long", so the line says "their capital" and the label prints TENOCHTITLAN. "Olmecs", "Aztecs", "Hidalgo", "Diego Rivera" all pass. No "live".
