# Turkey in 60 Seconds — Treatment

Series: History in 60 Seconds (@PrintedHistory) · Silkscreen Travel Poster · 1080×1920, 24 fps, 58.3 s.

| | Mexico | France | Turkey |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | a hidden-city mystery | "imagine…" | **a challenge** ("Can we fit nearly 12,000 years of Turkey into one minute? Let's find out.") |
| Cold open | spring pull-back | torch reveal | **a whip pan**: close on Hagia Sophia, a fast sideways pan that overshoots and settles, then an ease out to the whole print |
| Base | agave fields | Provence lavender | **Cappadocia**: fairy chimneys with dark caps and cave windows, a terraced slope of apricot trees swaying, cloud bars, a sun high on the left, storks wheeling |
| Signature | papel picado | the wall falls block by block | **Iznik tiles**: in 1923 the screen is grouted into tiles and, in a diagonal wave, each tile flips over (its glazed back showing a rosette as it turns) to reveal the new print; it plays in its own pause |
| Silence | 1521 | 52 BC | 1453: the music stops on "cannons"; one davul stroke and a low bassoon note fade; the line gets a 0.8 s pause so the silence sweep runs clear of speech |
| Comeback | 1810 | 1789 | 1923: the harp climbs one note per wave-front of tiles, a full chord with davul as the last tile turns; the mode turns major |
| Instruments | marimba, sesquiáltera | harpsichord, strings, 3/4 | **oboe as zurna, harp as kanun, bassoon drone, davul and frame drum** — real samples (CC0) — in **9/8 aksak (2+2+2+3)**; D Hicaz → D major from 1923 |
| Ending | "Have you been to Mexico?" | "Which print is your favourite?" | **"Have you tried Turkish tea? Tell us below."** — card "TEA / TIME?" |

Eras: c. 9500 BC Göbekli Tepe (a ring of T-pillars carved with a fox, a boar and a bird, hunters, running gazelles, vultures) · c. 1600 BC Hattusa (walls and towers on a ridge, a corbelled gate, a chariot with turning wheels and dust) · 330 Constantinople (a great column with a gilded statue, domes, sea walls, galleys rowing) · 537 Hagia Sophia (the dome on its ring of windows, half-domes, cypresses, ships) · 1453 (the triple walls breached, smoke, a great cannon firing) · 1500s (a mosque with cascading domes and four minarets, tulips, caiques) · 1923 Ankara (the castle on its hill, a stone assembly hall, a boulevard with lamps and young trees) · today (the strait, a suspension bridge, a mosque, a tower, ferries, balloons) · the wall of prints.

Engine changes: `cold.whip` [x from, x to, y, zoom] and `tiles: true` (film.js `tilesWipe`, 1.2 s sweep in a 1.1 s pause; timeline.py treats it like the other signature wipes); ZK widened to 1.0 / 1.14 (more of the frame changes each second); a silence era can take a `pause` too.

Voice notes: "Göbekli Tepe" is spelled "Gurbeckly Teppay" (/ɡɜːbɛkli tɛpeɪ/), "Hattusa" "Hattoosa" (/hatuːsə/; whisper writes "Hatusa"), "Hagia Sophia" "Haggia Sofia" (/haɡiə səʊfiə/; "Aya Sofia" came back as "Iosophia"). "the Hittites rule from…" was heard as "ruled": the line says "make Hattusa their walled capital". "rules an empire" was heard as "rules and empire": "rules a vast empire". "Ataturk", "Suleiman", "Ankara", "Constantinople" pass. No "live".
