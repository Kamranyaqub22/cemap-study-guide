## Candidate structures
1. **Fact first, then rewind (chosen):** the hook claims the impossible-sounding contrast, the print rewinds to 4,000 years ago, and the film earns the claim era by era. It ends back at the tower, which closes the loop for Shorts replays.
2. **Count the emirates:** seven prints, one per emirate. Too much geography and not enough history.
3. **Pearl to oil:** the whole film is the pearl-to-oil turn. Strong, but it drops the deep past.

## Beats (final times follow the narration)
Hook (tower close-up → pull-back) · Magan copper trade, round stone tomb and reed boat · 7th century, mosque and palms · 1500s, Portuguese carrack · 1820, fort and wind towers · 1930s, dhow and a pearl, the music stops · 1958, offshore platform with its flare, the comeback hit · 1971, seven palms in seven inks, a fountain sweep and a snap on "unite" · Today, night skyline, the tower, a probe beside a red Mars · the wall of eight prints, "Which country should we print next?".

## Sound
Room tone and paper swish on every re-ink, never hiss. Desert wind for the inland eras, waves and gulls on the coast, creaking timber for ships, a low gas roar under the oil flare, city hum at night. The voice sits ~10 dB above the music; −14 LUFS.

## Safe zones and motion
Text stays clear of the Shorts top bar, the right button column and the bottom caption area. Something moves in every era: haze bands and birds drift, the sea glints, ships sail, the flare flickers, windows blink, the probe drifts. No frozen stretch of 2 s or more.
