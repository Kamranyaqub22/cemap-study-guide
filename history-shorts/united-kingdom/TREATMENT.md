Eras: 2500 BC Stonehenge (trilithons, dawn light in the great gap, a crew hauling a stone on rollers) · AD 122 Hadrian's Wall (the wall riding the crags, a milecastle, soldiers marching, rain) · 1066 the Norman Conquest (a stone keep on its mound, torches in the slits, riders) · 1599 Shakespeare's Globe (a round timber playhouse with a thatched roof by the river, a wherry) · 1707 Great Britain (highland peaks and rolling hills joined by a stone bridge, a coach crossing, a thistle and a rose side by side) · 1830 the first intercity line (mills smoking, a brick viaduct, a steam train, rain) · 1940 the Blitz (night, a great dome above the smoke, searchlights swinging, fires, barrage balloons) · today (a turning observation wheel, a Gothic clock tower, glass towers, a red bus on the bridge) · the wall of prints.

Engine additions (film.js): parts can `loop` (rain tile), `spin` (the wheel, the clock hands) and `swing` (searchlights); `cold.push` + `cold.roll` for the push-in; `steam: true` on an era replaces its squeegee with the engine wipe (timeline.py gives it a 1.2 s sweep starting 1.0 s before the year).
