# USA in 60 Seconds — Treatment

Series: History in 60 Seconds (@PrintedHistory) · Silkscreen Travel Poster · 1080×1920, 24 fps, 53.1 s.

| | United Kingdom | India | USA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | peak first, then rewind | talks to the viewer about something they use | **a quiz question** ("Quick quiz: which country has the oldest written constitution still in use?"), answered in 1787 and asked back at the end |
| Cold open | push in with a roll | descend in an arc | **a punch-in**: a fast zoom onto the Art Deco tower's spire, then a slow release to the whole print |
| Base | chalk downs, hedged fields | monsoon sky, rice terraces, banyan | **prairie under a big sky**: a towering cumulus, a sun with turning ray stripes, a snowy mountain range, golden grass that waves, a fence line, a turning windmill pump, a hawk wheeling |
| Signature | steam-engine wipe | brush-painted zero | **the rocket-plume wipe**: in 1969 a rocket climbs the screen and its exhaust billows roll up behind it, wiping in the launch; it plays in its own pause before the line |
| Silence | 1940 | 1858 | 1861: the band stops on "splits"; a lone fiddle note falls over the wind |
| Comeback | today's peal | 1930 | 1903: a fiddle run up and a full banjo chord as the plane lifts off |
| Instruments | bells, brass, snare 4/4 | sitar, tabla, tanpura | **banjo (forward rolls), fiddle (bowed), upright bass, washboard**, a 2/4 bluegrass breakdown in G major (E minor for 1861) |
| Ending | "Which era would you visit?" | "What surprised you most?" | **"Did you get the quiz right? Tell us below."** — card "GOT IT / RIGHT?" |

Eras: 1050 Cahokia (a terraced platform mound with a timber hall, smaller mounds, a palisade, a ring of posts, canoes on the river) · 1776 (a brick hall with a white steeple and a swinging bell, a crowd, a coach) · 1787 (a parchment headed "We the People", lines written one by one, signatures, a quill, candles) · 1861 (fog, a cannon, a broken rail fence, a bare tree, crows) · 1903 (dunes by the sea, a biplane lifting off, a man running) · 1931 (an Art Deco tower, a blimp, a crane, rooftop steam) · 1969 (a rocket climbing above its tower, a crowd on the causeway) · today (a red suspension bridge in rolling fog, cars, sailboats, a hill city) · the wall of prints.

Engine changes: `rocket: true` (film.js `rocketWipe`, 1.2 s sweep); `cold.punch`; the camera is part linear and keeps drifting under the next sweep (no still frames at era ends); each print lands 0.1 s before its year is spoken and lines are 0.45 s apart, so no narration sound falls inside a sweep (the hiss check).

Voice notes: "still in force today" came back as "still enforced today": the line says "still the law today". "Cahokia", "Mississippi", "Apollo 11" all pass. No "live".
