# Reference: "Spain Train Crash: How Did Two Trains Collide?" (Tuesdayat3)

Reviewed 2026-11-16 from captions + storyboard thumbnails (the video download was blocked).
2 min 36 s Short · 1.5 M views, 46 k likes, 973 comments in 5 days · channel 7.4 k subscribers · uploaded 19 days after the event.

**Why it works**
- Hook gives the outcome first ("by the time the driver sees the train ahead, it is already too late"), so the viewer watches for *how*.
- A clock structure: "Wednesday morning…", "At 8:31 the system fails", "8:48".
- Real radio calls quoted; the repeated "At normal speed." is the dramatic beat.
- Cutting between two points of view (stopped red train / approaching white train; control room / cab / passengers).
- A terrain-board "map" with floating labels showing the gap closing, instead of a real map.
- A factual, respectful ending (casualties, investigation ongoing, minister resigned).
- Fresh news people were searching for.

**How it's made:** low-poly game-engine 3D (trains, cab, control room, cartoon characters, cabin), shots of about 2–3 s, slow pushes and
tracking shots, 2–4-word white captions centred low, a calm documentary voice at roughly 140–150 words a minute.

**Our version:** "Minute by Minute", low-poly three.js reconstructions of *historical* disasters and close calls (Titanic, Tay Bridge,
Hindenburg, Apollo 13, Halifax Explosion, Gimli Glider…). Demo: `new-channel/demos/demo_titanic.mp4`.
Avoid recent tragedies (accuracy, real people, ad limits) and real recorded audio (rights).
